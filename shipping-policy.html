Shipping Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

Dispatch

Our current dispatch time is 1–2 business days. Dispatch time is the time before an order leaves us; it is separate from carrier transit time.

United States shipping

US shipping is calculated by USPS from your address and the packed weight of your order. The available services and the final shipping charge are shown at checkout before you pay.

Shipping is free on US orders over $75.

International shipping

We ship to international destinations offered at checkout. Available services, delivery estimates, and the full charge for your address are shown before payment. Some destinations may require a phone number, and orders to mainland China may require a Resident ID for customs.

Delays and delivery

Carrier transit estimates begin after dispatch and are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. If we learn that an order cannot ship within the promised time, we will contact you with the updated timing and available options.

Questions

For a shipping question, email asiorclothing@gmail.com with your order number.
