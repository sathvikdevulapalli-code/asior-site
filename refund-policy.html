Fit and order issues

If the fit is not what you expected, your order is incorrect, an item arrives damaged, or something else is wrong, email us so we can review it with you. We consider these requests case by case.

Report a problem

Email asiorclothing@gmail.com as soon as you notice a problem. Include your order number and clear photos of the item and its packaging so we can review it. Please wait for our reply before sending anything back.

Replacements and refunds

A replacement is usually the first solution we offer. If another solution is needed, email us and we can discuss it. A refund may be arranged when you and our support team agree to it by email. We will review your report and send next steps. Do not mail an item to us without instructions from our support team.

Questions

Contact asiorclothing@gmail.com and include your order number.
